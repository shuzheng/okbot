---
name: "okbot-fix-bug"
description: "Finds the cause of a failure and gives a minimal fix. Use when the user pastes an error, the program does not run, or the result is wrong. Do NOT use for: pure feature design with no failure, or unrelated requirements chat."
---

1. Restate the symptom and the key part of the error text.
2. Give one or two most likely causes.
3. Give a minimal fix as steps or a patch.
4. Say how to check that the fix worked.
